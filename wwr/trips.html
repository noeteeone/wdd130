Trip Types & Durations

Half-Day Trips: Focus on a shorter section of the gorge often covering early rapids like 1 through 5

Full-Day Trips: Run a larger sequence of the pool-drop rapids with lunch served either down in the gorge or at the top after exiting.

Multi-Day Expeditions: Ranging from overnight up to 4, 5, 8, or 10 days, allowing you to camp on white sandy river beaches deep inside the gorge.
