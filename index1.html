Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is a landlocked country in southern Africa, that is bordered by Zambia to the North, Mozambique to the East, South Africa to the South, Botswana to the West and Namibia to the North West. It has a population of approximately 15 million people. English, Shona and Ndebele are the widely spoken languages. Its capital city is Harare and the second largest city is Bulawayo. It is home to one of the seven wonders of the world, the Victoria Falls on the Zambezi river.
